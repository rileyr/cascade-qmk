Start a job, then put the board in the bootloader. On stock firmware: back switch on USB, cable out, short the two right-most of the five pads left of the spacebar switch with tweezers, plug in while shorted, release. On QMK: hold Fn and tap Esc. The job flashes as soon as the bootloader appears and the keyboard reboots on its own.
